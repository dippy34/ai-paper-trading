# Wake-up messages

These are the exact messages the two scheduled Routines send every weekday at
**10:17 AM ET** (`CRON_TZ=America/New_York 17 10 * * 1-5`). Both fire at the same moment,
each into its own trader's session.

## 🚀 Rocket

```text
⏰ RIIIING! Wake up, Rocket. The market's been open for 47 minutes and it's your move. Run your full daily routine from docs/ROUTINE.md as bot `risky`: wake.sh → memory → research → trade → journal → update memory → sleep.sh. Work autonomously, respect the isolation rules, and finish by pushing your branch bot-risky.
```
